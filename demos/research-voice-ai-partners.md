# Voice-AI-for-Sales: Investor Deal Leads, Cheque Sizes & Stage Profiles

## Executive Summary

This memo synthesises all available evidence on the five firms—Y Combinator, Base10 Partners, Emergence Capital, First Harmonic, and Peak XV Partners—covering named deal leads for each specified portfolio company, typical cheque sizes, and stage focus. The most granular lead attribution comes from First Harmonic (J.J. Fliegelman confirmed as lead on Simple AI) and Emergence Capital (Yaz El-Baba and Gordon Ritter confirmed as the Bland backers). Base10's specific deal-lead partners for Flai and Phonely are **not publicly disclosed** in any available source. The YC lead on Simple AI resolves to Diana Hu as Simple AI's listed Primary Partner on YC's own platform. Arnav Sahu is confirmed as Peak XV's US investing partner and the most credible attribution for the Vapi Series B, though Peak XV has not published a named deal lead.

---

## 1. Y Combinator — Simple AI

### Deal Lead

Y Combinator participated in Simple AI's $14 million round (February 2026) as a **non-lead** investor. The round was led by First Harmonic [17][40]. YC's own company listing for Simple AI records **Diana Hu** as the **Primary Partner** for Simple AI within YC's program [12]. YC's standard program deal does not involve a single "partner who leads" in the same sense as a traditional VC check—every accepted company is backed by the program collectively, with a named primary partner assigned for mentorship.

> **Bottom line:** Diana Hu is the named Primary YC Partner on Simple AI's YC page [12]. YC then participated (non-lead) in the $14M external round alongside First Harmonic [17]. No source names a YC partner who "led" YC's follow-on cheque in the external round.

### Cheque Size & Stage Profile

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Standard program deal | $500,000 total per company: $125K on a post-money SAFE for 7% equity + $375K on an uncapped MFN SAFE [11] |
| Stage | Pre-seed/seed (batch intake), with pro-rata follow-on rights exercised into later rounds [11] |
| Follow-on | YC holds pro-rata rights and "in many cases has invested millions of dollars" in later rounds via YC Continuity [11] |
| Phonely, Bland AI, Flai, Vapi | YC participated (non-lead) in Phonely's Series A [7], Bland AI's Series A and Series B [2], Flai's Series A [9], and Vapi's Series B [6]—all as a continuation of pro-rata rights, not as round lead |

## 2. Base10 Partners — Flai & Phonely

### Deal Leads

Base10 **led** both rounds:
- **Flai**: $27M Series A, announced October 2026, led by Base10 Partners [9][10]
- **Phonely**: $16M Series A, April 2026, led by Base10 Partners [7][39]

**However, no public source names the specific Base10 partner who led either deal.** Base10's team pages [25][26] and all press coverage [9][10][7][39][41] attribute the investment to the firm without naming an individual partner. Base10 was co-founded by **Adeyemi Ajao** and **TJ Nahigian** (both Managing Partners) [27], and Ajao is known to take board seats at portfolio companies [27], but no source confirms he or any named partner specifically led the Flai or Phonely deals. This is a genuine evidence gap.

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Seed check | $2M–$8M (Fund 4 seed vehicle) [14][27] |
| Series A check | $5M–$15M (Fund 4) [14] |
| Series B check | Larger, via dedicated Series B Fund 2; example: led $44M Series B in HappyRobot [14] |
| Blended range reported | $2M–$10M across stages [27] |
| Stage focus | Seed through Series B; plans 10–15 seed investments and 2–3 Series A investments per year from early-stage fund; 3–4 Series B investments per year from Series B fund [41] |
| AUM | $2.6B total across all funds, following June 2026 close of $850M across two new funds [27][41] |

Both Flai (automotive dealership AI) and Phonely (business voice AI) fit Base10's real-economy automation thesis squarely [9][7].

## 3. Emergence Capital — Bland AI & Veritus

### Deal Leads

**Bland AI:** The named Emergence partners are **Yaz El-Baba** and **Gordon Ritter**. Emergence's own Series C post states: *"When Yaz El-Baba and Gordon Ritter first backed Bland, the bet was that voice would become a serious enterprise infrastructure layer"* [31]. Yaz El-Baba is listed as the investor contact on Bland's portfolio page at emcap.com [28]. Yaz El-Baba is a Partner (promoted to Partner in 2024, joined as Associate in 2019) [29][30]; Gordon Ritter is a co-founder of Emergence Capital [35].

The investment history across Bland's rounds:
- **Series A** ($16M, August 2024): Led by Scale Venture Partners, with YC and angels participating; Emergence not listed as lead [2]

The round was led by **Crosslink and Threshold**, with Emergence Capital, Surge Point, Cedar Capital, and Rebel Fund as supporters [8]. No specific Emergence partner is named in connection with Veritus in any available source.

## 5. Peak XV Partners — Vapi

### Deal Lead

**Arnav Sahu** is Peak XV's US investing partner and is the most credible attribution for the Vapi Series B lead. He is the **first and only Partner-titled person** focused on US deals at Peak XV [22][36], having previously spent four years at Y Combinator leading seed-to-growth investments [20][36]. Peak XV's portfolio page lists Vapi as a Growth-stage company first partnered in 2026 [16][21]. However, **Peak XV has not publicly named Arnav Sahu as the deal lead for Vapi**—the Series B announcement does not name a specific partner [5][6], and Peak XV's team and company pages do not attribute the deal [21][22].

The Vapi round:
- **Amount:** $50M Series B (May 2026)
- **Lead:** Peak XV Partners [5][6]
- **Valuation:** ~$500M post-money [5]
- **Participants:** YC, Microsoft M12, Kleiner Perkins, Bessemer Venture Partners [5][6]

> **Confidence note:** Arnav Sahu as deal lead is a reasonable and well-supported inference (he is Peak XV's sole named US Partner [22], Vapi is a US-based company, and the round was announced in his active investment period) but is **not explicitly confirmed** in any public source. Use as working attribution pending direct confirmation.

## Key Observations for Decision-Makers

**1. Base10's deal-lead opacity is structural, not accidental.** The firm's two Managing Partners (Ajao, Nahigian) [27] appear to share deal responsibility without systematic public attribution by company. Any diligence conversation will need to be initiated directly to establish board seat holder and primary relationship owner for Flai and Phonely.

**2. Emergence's Bland relationship is unusually transparent.** The firm published a named thesis post ("AI That Speaks Volumes") [1] and a named follow-on post naming El-Baba and Ritter [31], indicating a high-conviction, long-duration relationship across Series B and C. This is rare for Emergence, which makes only 5–7 investments per year [13].

**3. First Harmonic's cheque on Simple AI is atypically large.** The firm's standard range is $150K–$2M [32][38], yet it led a $14M round [17][40]. This signals either a co-lead structure where others provided most capital, or a significant departure from First Harmonic's typical participation size—the $14M is the total round size, not necessarily First Harmonic's cheque alone. The co-investors (YC, Massive Tech, True Ventures) [17] likely contributed the majority of capital.

**4. Peak XV's US expansion is nascent but well-resourced.** With $9B AUM [20] and a newly elevated Surge seed ceiling [$5M per company] [33], Peak XV can write competitive cheques at any stage. The Vapi Series B at $50M at a $500M valuation [5] is the largest individual cheque among this group and signals Peak XV's willingness to lead significant growth rounds in the US.

**5. YC's role across this portfolio is consistently that of a small, non-lead participant** exercising pro-rata rights from its standard $500K program deal [11], visible in Bland AI, Flai, Phonely, and Vapi [2][9][7][6].
